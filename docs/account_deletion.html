MatricET Account Support

Request Account and Data Deletion

At MatricET, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal account and associated learning data. This page outlines how you can delete your account both directly inside the app and via a web request.

Method 1: Direct In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

If you have the MatricET application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the MatricET app and sign in to your account.
  2. Navigate to Profile (bottom navigation menu).
  3. Tap on Edit Profile / Settings.
  4. Scroll down to the Account Actions section and tap Delete Account.
  5. Enter your current password to confirm your identity.
  6. Confirm deletion. Your account, sessions, test history, bookmarks, and payment receipts are immediately and permanently removed from our servers.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device:

Submit a deletion request via email:

Send an email to: [INSERT_YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE]
Subject: MatricET Account Deletion Request
Body: Please include the email address registered with your MatricET account.

Requests are processed within 7 business days of verification.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion, all personal information linked to your user record is permanently purged via database cascade:

  • User Profile: Name, email address, password credentials, and stream preferences.
  • Device & Session Data: Device identifier links and session state.
  • Learning Progress: Exam attempts, question scores, saved test drafts, and statistics.
  • Bookmarks: All bookmarked questions and passages.
  • Payment Records: Uploaded payment receipt image files and pending payment requests.
  • Notifications: Personal notification history and read receipts.

Data Retention

We do not retain any residual personal data once deletion completes. Anonymized, aggregated statistics that cannot identify any individual student may be kept for educational content quality benchmarking.
